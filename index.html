Westworld Finance Diligence Bench Audited leaderboard: expert spot-checks turned into calibrated scores

Concept · November 2026

Every score on the leaderboard is a verifier grade, and most of a task's weight sits on agentic checks that can pass work an expert would fail. This view takes the expert audits you already run, picks which attempts to audit next, and turns the audits into an unbiased score for every configuration with a 95% interval, plus a ranked list of the verifiers most likely to be passing bad work.
